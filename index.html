01 / The big priority

Get a home, with a plan.

Rentals can move quickly. Prepare one strong application and search across several channels before you arrive.

01

Define your search

Set a monthly budget including charges, your move-in date and non-negotiables. Compare commute times on the CTS network, including nearby Eurométropole towns.

02

Prepare your dossier

Collect the documents a landlord can legally request. DossierFacile helps create a shareable rental file. Check whether Visale can act as guarantor.

03

Search, visit, verify

Try agencies, direct listings, flatshares and Maison de l'habitat. Visit before committing when possible; check the lease, energy rating, charges and inventory.

02 / Arrival essentials

Your first week, made simpler.

Some steps depend on your nationality, employment and household. Use the official pages to check your situation.

01 / ADMIN

Register your essentials

Use the city's newcomer guide for local services. For visas, residence, health cover, taxes and other national procedures, start at Service-Public.fr.

02 / HOME

Set up the address

Arrange home insurance before moving in, then utilities and internet. Keep copies of the signed lease, entry inventory and payment receipts.

03 / MOVEMENT

Learn the routes

Use CTS for tram, bus, tickets and live changes. Explore cycle options through Vélhop.

04 / FAMILY

School and care

If arriving with children, check school and after-school registration. Find local healthcare information through the city and national services.
